# Personal Budget

Terms for planning monthly money, recording transactions, and reviewing financial progress.

## Language

**Included tag**:
A tag a transaction must have at least one of when included-tag filters are selected.
_Avoid_: Required tag

**Excluded tag**:
A tag that removes a transaction from report results when the transaction has it. If a tag is both included and excluded, the exclusion applies.
_Avoid_: Omitted tag

**Savings**:
Money set aside for future use.

**Savings goal**:
The target retirement savings balance used to measure financial progress.

**Year-end retirement balance**:
The total retirement savings balance at the end of a calendar year.
_Avoid_: Annual contributions, amount saved that year

**Savings milestone**:
A retirement savings amount marking either an achieved milestone or a milestone target.

**Achieved milestone**:
A retirement savings milestone the user has reached, with a record of its month and year. It remains an achievement if the retirement balance later falls.

**Milestone target**:
A retirement savings amount the user intends to reach, optionally by a target month and year. Reaching its amount does not establish when the achievement happened.

**Budget**:
A plan that assigns expected income to planned spending and savings for a period.
_Avoid_: Actual spending, transaction history

**Transaction**:
A recorded movement of money, such as income or an expense, with a date and category.
_Avoid_: Budget item, planned amount

**Planned amount**:
The amount assigned to an income or expense category in a budget.
_Avoid_: Actual, transaction amount

**Actual amount**:
The amount represented by recorded transactions for a category and period.
_Avoid_: Planned amount

**Flow path**:
The ordered groups that place a planned expense within a budget flow. The expense name identifies the final item within those groups.

**Comparison inclusion**:
Whether a planned expense contributes to an actual-versus-budget comparison. An excluded expense remains part of the budget and its flow.
